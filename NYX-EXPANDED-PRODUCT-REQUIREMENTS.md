NYX — EXPANDED PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

The Creative Experience, Intelligent Studio Routing, Social Platform and Navigation

Product: NYX — The Creative Universe
Tagline: Imagine beyond. Create everything.
Document status: Companion to "NYX-ARCHITECTURE.md"

This document expands the original NYX architecture. It does not replace the original blueprint. Both documents must be followed during development.

---

1. THE NYX EXPERIENCE

NYX must feel like entering a complete creative universe.

It should be:

- Elegant, futuristic, premium and welcoming.
- Easy to understand, even for first-time users.
- Visually organised, not crowded.
- Designed for mobile phones first, while supporting tablets and computers.
- Built around natural conversation rather than complicated menus.

The first introduction should explain what NYX does and make users feel that they have entered a place where they can bring creative ideas to life.

Use the official tagline:

Imagine beyond. Create everything.

Do not use random stock portraits or a decorative woman image as the main introduction.

The visual identity should use purposeful creative artwork, thoughtful typography, balanced spacing and a consistent colour system.

2. THE HOME PAGE LAYOUT

The home page must be carefully staged into clear sections.

A. Welcome and introduction

- NYX logo and identity.
- A warm introduction explaining what the platform can do.
- A clear way to begin creating.
- Account access.
- A clean, visually memorable first impression.

B. The NYX conversation area

This is the central way users communicate with NYX.

It must include:

- A visible typing area.
- A send button.
- A voice-note recording button.
- Recording and stop controls.
- A way to review and send a recorded voice note.
- Clear recording permissions and error messages.
- A conversation display showing the user's messages and NYX responses.
- Appropriate loading and processing states.

Users must be able to describe what they want in their own words. They should not be forced to learn studio names or technical commands.

Example:

"I want to create a romantic short film with original background music."

NYX should understand the request and coordinate the appropriate studios.

C. The 12 studio directory

All 12 studios must be visible from the beginning of the experience.

Each studio must have:

- Its own distinctive visual thumbnail or artwork.
- A clearly readable studio name.
- A short explanation of what it does.
- A clear entry point into its workflow.
- A consistent card design.

The layout must be spacious and carefully arranged.

On narrow phones, use two columns where appropriate. On wider phones, tablets and desktops, adapt the number of columns to the available space.

Do not squeeze all 12 studios into tiny cards to fit one screen. Users can scroll.

Every studio must remain identifiable and easy to select.

D. Creation preview

Include a dedicated area for users to preview their creative work.

It must:

- Display genuine generated or imported results when available.
- Show project status where appropriate.
- Provide access to saved projects.
- Have a useful empty state before the user creates anything.

Never show a fake generated image, song or video as if it were a real result.

E. Main navigation

NYX must have simple, consistent navigation to:

1. Home
2. Create
3. Reels
4. Inbox
5. Profile

Settings and project access must also be easy to find.

The navigation must work on mobile and desktop. It must not cover important content or become confusing.

3. THE INTELLIGENT NYX CREATIVE ASSISTANT

This is one of the most important parts of the entire platform.

NYX must understand the creator's intention and select the appropriate studio or combination of studios.

Users should not have to figure out which studio they need before they can begin.

How it works

1. The creator types or speaks an idea.
2. NYX interprets the request.
3. NYX identifies the intended creative result.
4. NYX determines which studio or studios are required.
5. NYX opens or activates the correct studio workflow.
6. NYX asks only necessary follow-up questions.
7. The creator provides instructions and makes creative decisions.
8. The selected studio performs its actual supported work.
9. NYX presents the real result or explains what remains unavailable.
10. The creator can save, continue editing, export or publish where supported.

The system must preserve the creator's original intention when moving between studios.

Intelligent studio routing examples

Request: "Create an advertisement for my clothing business."

Route to:

- Marketing & Advertising.
- Video Editing if the creator wants a video advertisement.
- Music & Beat Production if original music is requested.

Request: "Write a fantasy story and make it an audiobook."

Route to:

- Storytelling & Voice.
- Audiobook Production.

Request: "Create a character and put them into this video."

Route to:

- Character Creation.
- Step Into The Scene.
- Video Editing if additional editing is required.

Request: "Make an original gengetone beat."

Route to:

- Music & Beat Production.

Request: "Create a short comedy skit."

Route to:

- Comedy & Humour.
- Video Editing if the creator wants a finished video.

Request: "Make a film with characters, dialogue and a soundtrack."

Route to:

- Film & Movie Production.
- Storytelling & Voice.
- Character Creation.
- Soundtrack & Background Music.
- Video Editing where required.

Routing rules

- Do not automatically open an unrelated studio.
- Do not force users to choose a studio when NYX can confidently understand their intention.
- When a request is unclear, ask one useful clarification.
- For complex projects, explain which studios will work together.
- Allow users to change the selected studio or workflow.
- Allow users to request additional studios during an existing project.
- Keep project context and assets connected across studios.
- Never claim a studio has completed work when it has not.
- If a required engine is unavailable, explain the limitation and offer a supported alternative.

The creator remains in control. NYX assists and coordinates; it does not make important creative decisions against the creator's wishes.

4. THE 12 STUDIOS AND THEIR FUNCTIONS

All 12 studios must have distinct identities and actual workflows.

Studio 1 — Marketing & Advertising

- Marketing campaigns.
- Brand concepts and strategies.
- Advertisements.
- Promotional graphics and content.
- Social media marketing.
- Product and business promotions.

Studio 2 — Video Editing

- Import videos.
- Arrange and trim clips.
- Add transitions.
- Add text and subtitles.
- Add effects.
- Add music and sound.
- Adjust timing.
- Preview and export finished videos.

Studio 3 — Film & Movie Production

- Film concepts.
- Screenplays and scripts.
- Scene planning.
- Storyboards.
- Character and location planning.
- Production workflows.
- Video production coordination.

Studio 4 — Music & Beat Production

- Original beats.
- Original songs and instrumentals.
- Music across global genres.
- African regional styles, including gengetone, urbantone and mugithi.
- Layered production.
- Instrument and sound design.
- Mixing and mastering.
- Audio export.

Studio 5 — Game Creation

- Game concepts.
- Gameplay planning.
- Characters.
- Environments.
- Game assets.
- Development workflows.
- Testing and iteration.

Studio 6 — Storytelling & Voice

- Stories and novels.
- Books.
- Scripts and dialogue.
- Creative writing.
- Character dialogue.
- Voice interaction and narration workflows.

Studio 7 — Audiobook Production

- Import or select written work.
- Organise chapters.
- Select supported narration voices.
- Generate narration using a connected engine.
- Preview audio.
- Export downloadable audiobook files.

Studio 8 — Character Creation

- Character appearance.
- Personality.
- Background and history.
- Character profiles.
- Consistent character identity across projects.
- Character assets for stories, films, games and other creative work.

Studio 9 — Soundtrack & Background Music

- Original film soundtracks.
- Background music.
- Atmospheric audio.
- Emotional and dramatic music.
- Scene-specific sound.
- Export and integration with video projects.

Studio 10 — Step Into The Scene

- Insert selected characters into existing video clips and scenes.
- Preserve visual consistency where supported.
- Provide creator controls over placement and appearance.
- Respect copyright, ownership and permission requirements.

Studio 11 — Comedy & Humour

- Jokes.
- Comedy scripts.
- Skits.
- Funny characters.
- Humorous stories.
- Comedy concepts and performance ideas.
- Coordination with video and audio studios.

Studio 12 — Import & Export

- Import supported files.
- Validate file types and sizes.
- Organise imported assets.
- Export finished projects.
- Preserve project information.
- Avoid unnecessary quality loss.
- Explain unsupported formats clearly.

5. CONNECTED CREATIVE PROJECTS

The studios must work together rather than operate as twelve isolated tools.

A project may involve several studios.

For example, a film project could contain:

- Its script from Storytelling & Voice.
- Its characters from Character Creation.
- Its scenes from Film & Movie Production.
- Its soundtrack from Soundtrack & Background Music.
- Its final edit from Video Editing.

All connected work must remain organised under the same project.

Users must be able to move between studios without losing their progress.

6. REELS — THE SOCIAL VIDEO EXPERIENCE

Reels is a core part of NYX.

It is not optional and must not be removed from the product vision.

The Reels page must provide:

- A full-screen vertical video feed.
- For You and Following feeds.
- Smooth vertical scrolling.
- Real video playback.
- Creator name and handle.
- Captions.
- Audio information and attribution.
- Like and unlike.
- Comments and replies.
- Save and unsave.
- Repost and undo repost.
- Share.
- Follow and unfollow.
- Creator profile access.
- Search and discovery.
- Report and safety controls.

All social actions must eventually persist in the backend. They must not be fake animations or local-only counters.

Save and repost distinction

Save:

- Private to the user.
- Visible in the user's saved collection.
- Does not publicly publish the saved content.

Repost:

- Publicly shares the original creator's post with attribution.
- Must retain a link to the original content.
- Must be reversible where supported.

Sharing must not silently publish private projects.

7. CREATOR PROFILES

Each account should have a creator profile containing:

- Profile photo or avatar.
- Display name.
- Unique username or handle.
- Bio.
- Optional contact information or links controlled by the creator.
- Follower count.
- Following count.
- Likes count.
- Follow and unfollow.
- Message action subject to privacy settings.
- Pinned videos.
- A three-column public video grid where screen size allows.
- Access to public posts.

Profile owners must control which information is public.

Private contact details must not be exposed by default.

8. INBOX AND MESSAGING

NYX must include an Inbox for private conversations.

Initial functions:

- Conversation list.
- Text messaging.
- Message timestamps.
- Delivery status where supported.
- Opening conversations from profiles.
- Privacy settings.
- Blocking.
- Reporting.

Voice notes and media attachments can be added when their actual services are implemented.

Do not display controls as functional before their underlying service exists.

9. NOTIFICATIONS AND DISCOVERY

Notifications should support:

- New followers.
- Likes.
- Comments.
- Replies.
- Mentions.
- Reposts where appropriate.
- Direct messages.
- Important account and safety notices.

Discovery should help users find creators and public content while respecting account privacy and content visibility settings.

10. ACCOUNTS AND PRIVACY

NYX must support multiple independent user accounts.

Requirements:

- Secure registration and sign-in.
- Private projects.
- Individual asset ownership.
- Private saved content.
- Private messages.
- Server-side permission checks.
- Secure sessions.
- Rate limits and abuse prevention.
- Blocking and reporting.
- Account and content deletion procedures.
- Backup and recovery planning.

Test with at least two separate accounts.

One user must not be able to access another user's private projects, messages, saved content or account information.

A hidden button or frontend restriction is not security.

11. CONTENT RIGHTS AND SAFETY

- Users must have permission to upload, edit, publish or reuse content.
- Preserve creator attribution.
- Do not present copyrighted music or movie audio as freely reusable.
- Provide original or appropriately licensed alternatives where possible.
- Include reporting and blocking.
- Keep private drafts private.
- Apply appropriate content and messaging safeguards.
